Invitation to Participate:
The goal of this research is to develop new music generation models for video games. In this study, we want to evaluate the quality of music generated by artificial intelligence models compared to the original soundtracks of different games.


Study Objective:
With this research, we aim to determine whether our music generation systems can compose video game music from a specific gameplay video. We also want to evaluate the quality of the generated pieces compared to video game music composed by professional musicians.


Participation:
In this study, you will evaluate music generated for two different games. Both games are from the Super Nintendo Entertainment System (SNES). For each game, you will watch a video clip of that game with its original background music. After the original video, you will answer questions about your familiarity with the game you watched. Then, you will watch three different versions of the same video, each with a different generated background track. After watching each video with generated music, you will answer questions about the quality of the music heard. At the end, you will complete a demographic questionnaire. Each video, including the one with the original soundtrack, lasts 10 seconds. In total, you will watch videos. The full experiment takes approximately minutes. We recommend using headphones during this study.


Benefits:
There is no direct benefit for participants taking part in this study.


Risks:
The risks associated with this study are equivalent to those of listening to music on a computer.


Confidentiality and Anonymity:
The information you share will remain strictly confidential and will be used exclusively for the purposes of this research. The only people who will have access to the study data are the professor and the student involved in this study (see the names and contact information at the top of this page). Your responses to open-ended questions may be used in full in presentations and publications, but neither you nor your organization will be identified. To minimize the risk of security breaches and ensure your confidentiality, we recommend that you adopt standard security measures, such as closing the browser and locking your screen or device when you are no longer using it/when you finish the study. The results will be published in aggregate form. Anonymity is ensured because you will not be asked to provide your name or any personal information.


Voluntary Participation:
Participation is completely voluntary and, if you decide to take part, you may choose not to answer any questions you do not wish to. If you decide to stop during the electronic questionnaire, simply close the link and your answers will not be recorded. Due to the anonymous nature of the survey, once your responses are submitted, it will no longer be possible to remove them from the study.


Information About Study Results:
The results of our experiments will not be made directly available to participants.

Invitation to Participate:
You are invited to participate in this research study about music generation with Artificial Intelligence.


Purpose of the Study:
From this research, we wish to learn if our systems for music generation can compose video game music with a given target gameplay video. We also want to measure the quality of the generated pieces with respect to video game music composed by professional musicians.


Participation:
In this experiment, you will be asked to listen to video game music pieces with 10s of duration and evaluate the emotion and quality you perceive in each one of them. Each evaluation is approximately 45 seconds long. The entire experiment takes approximately minutes. We strongly recommend the use of headphones during this study.


Benefits:
There is no direct benefit to the participants taking part in the study.


Risks:
The risks associated with this study are equivalent to those of listening to music on a computer.


Confidentiality and Anonymity:
The information that you will share will remain strictly confidential and will be used solely for the purposes of this research. The only people who will have access to the research data are the professor and students involved in this research (see names and contact information at the top). Your answers to open-ended questions may be used verbatim in presentations and publications, but neither you (nor your organization) will be identified. In order to minimize the risk of security breaches and to help ensure your confidentiality, we recommend that you use standard safety measures such as closing your browser and locking your screen or device when you are no longer using them / when you have completed the study. Results will be published in pooled (aggregate) format. Anonymity is guaranteed since you are not being asked to provide your name or any personal information.


Voluntary Participation:
You are under no obligation to participate, and if you choose to participate, you may refuse to answer questions that you do not want to answer. Should you choose to withdraw midway through the electronic survey, simply close the link, and no responses will be included. Given the anonymous nature of the survey, once you have submitted your responses it will no longer be possible to withdraw them from the study.


Information about the Study Results:
The results of our experiments will not be available to the participants.


Contact Information:
If you have any questions or require more information about the study itself, you may contact the researchers at the e-mails mentioned herein.
